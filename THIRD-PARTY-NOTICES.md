# Third-party software

NearClip's update component includes [Sparkle 2.10.1](https://github.com/sparkle-project/Sparkle/releases/tag/2.10.1), an MIT-licensed macOS update framework. Its complete license and notices ship in `NearClipUpdater.app/Contents/Resources/Sparkle-LICENSE.txt` and the framework's resources.

The build downloads the official release archive and checks its pinned SHA-256 digest before extracting it. Sparkle source and licenses are available at [sparkle-project/Sparkle](https://github.com/sparkle-project/Sparkle).
